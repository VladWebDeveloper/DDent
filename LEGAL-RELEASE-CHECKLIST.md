# DDent — legal and release checklist, November 2026

**This is a non-indexed preview; code cannot guarantee absence of fines.** Obtain written review from the clinic and a qualified Moldovan lawyer before publishing on ddent.md.

- [ ] Confirm DDent's exact legal operator name, IDNO, registered address, administrator and contact information. Moldova Advertising Law 62/2022, Art. 26, requires business identification on online advertising platforms.
- [ ] Confirm in writing **24 years of professional practice** and **5,000+ treated patients**, attribution and evidence. Clinic founded 2016 on ddent.md; clinic age and dentist experience are distinct.
- [ ] Verify medical service availability, physician names/specializations, health advice in PDF and every claim.
- [ ] Reconcile Saturday hours: official site lists 09:00–15:00, Google lists 10:00–15:00.
- [ ] Document controller, data processors, legal basis, data retention, data subject rights, breach handling, security and cross-border transfers under Moldova Law 195/2024 (effective 23 Aug 2026).
- [ ] Do not collect sensitive medical details through an ordinary enquiry form. A future booking or medical CRM needs separate risk assessment and secure implementation.
- [ ] Implement an appropriate consent manager before adding non-essential analytics, behavioural advertising or cookies.
- [ ] Obtain written releases for identifiable patient photos/video/voices and clinical before/after, under applicable medical confidentiality, personal data, copyrights and Advertising Law 62/2022 Art. 6.
- [ ] Verify review rights, attributions and Google Maps access. Google Places API has limits on returned reviews (commonly 5); do not claim all reviews are in an official live API feed.
- [ ] Replace illustrative stock photography/video with authorized DDent media when representing the real premises/staff.
- [ ] Review and approve trilingual privacy, terms, and cookie disclosures. These are draft documents and explicitly identify missing legal details.
- [ ] Check responsive performance, Lighthouse, accessibility, heading hierarchy, keyboard navigation, links, security headers and remove noindex/robots restrictions only after client approval.
- [ ] Connect GitHub repository to Vercel if continuous deployment is required. Current preview uses direct Vercel API releases.
- [ ] Formalize hosting, maintenance, backups, access controls, administrator ownership and support process.

Sources: https://legi.md/LP_195-2024 ; https://legi.md/LP_62-2022 ; https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies ; https://ddent.md/ .
