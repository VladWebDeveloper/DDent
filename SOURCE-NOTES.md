# DDent Stomatologie — Editorial Concept

A bespoke one-page dental clinic website created for **DDent Stomatologie, Chișinău**.

## Features
- Cinematic 1080p MP4 hero with autoplay, mute, loop, playsinline and poster image
- Three-depth requestAnimationFrame scroll parallax; responsive; prefers-reduced-motion support
- Custom responsive visual identity, serif display typography, editorial layout and hand-drawn SVG logo
- Verified practice services, location, hours and tap-to-call phone
- Three verbatim review excerpts supplied with the client brief
- Contact links, Google Maps directions, schema.org/Dentist metadata, OpenGraph tags and accessible navigation

## Content / approval notes
**Preview only:** this is not the clinic's official site. The 1080p background video and photography are licensed **illustrative** media, not DDent premises, staff or patients. They should be replaced with authorized real DDent imagery before official launch. No private patient data is collected. No appointment calendar/CRM integration is implied. The bespoke AI-generated film and DDent PDF guide are not included because their source/approval is not available.
